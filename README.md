## 🙋 About Me

I am a **Ph.D. student** in Information and Communication Engineering at the [University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)](https://www.ustc.edu.cn/), advised by [**Prof. Li Xiao**](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=h3Wjbp8AAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). I began my graduate studies at USTC in 2024 and entered the Ph.D. program in September 2026. Before that, I received my B.E. in Communication Engineering from [Anhui University](https://www.ahu.edu.cn/) in 2024.

I am currently a research intern at **Qwen Business Unit of Alibaba**, working on **LLM post-training**.

My research interests include **Personal Agent**, **Agentic RL**, and **Agent Memory**. I also have research experience in **medical image analysis**.

My recent work includes [**ATPO**](https://github.com/Quark-Medical/ATPO) (**ICLR 2026 · Poster**), [**COPE**](https://github.com/Quark-Medical/COPE) (**Findings of EMNLP 2026**), [**MemCalib**](https://quark-medical.github.io/MemCalib-Project/), and [**RPMem**](https://quark-medical.github.io/RPMem/).
